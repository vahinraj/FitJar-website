LEGAL / YOUR DATA

Privacy Policy.

FitJar handles fitness and nutrition information that can be personal and sensitive. This page explains the information we need, why we use it, where it goes, and the choices available to you.

Last updated 27 September 2026 · India-first pre-launch draft

Pre-launch notice. This policy reflects the current product design and code review. Exact retention periods, minor consent arrangements, provider terms and final legal wording require review before the app launches publicly. The support page can be used for questions.

1. Who operates FitJar

FitJar is an application operated by Sirimalla Vahin Raj, an individual based in India. FitJar is a brand name and is not described here as a registered company. For privacy questions, requests, or complaints, email fitjar.support@gmail.com. India is the intended initial market. If availability expands, this policy will be reviewed for the new regions.

2. Information we handle

CategoryExamples and source
Account and identityEmail address, account identifier, sign-in method, authentication state, and account support messages. If you choose Google sign-in, Google provides the information needed to authenticate you.
Profile and goalsBirth date or age, height, weight, fitness goals, activity settings, language preferences, and other onboarding answers you choose to enter.
Fitness activityExercises, routines, sets, training dates, body measurements or check-ins, and app-generated training and recovery estimates.
Nutrition and hydrationMeals, ingredients or text descriptions, nutrition estimates, meal photos you select, water entries, and your corrections to estimates.

Technical informationApp version, device and platform details, diagnostic errors, limited service logs, and coarse usage information if enabled. We do not need your meal contents or exact body measurements in analytics events.

Some data is stored locally on your device for offline use. Signed-in account records are also stored in FitJar's cloud services so they can sync. Access to the device may expose local information if the device is not secured.

3. Why we use information

  • Provide the app: create an account, save and sync your entries, show training, nutrition and hydration history, and calculate views you request.
  • Personalize estimates: use profile information and logged activity to create calorie targets and Body Intelligence signals. These are wellness estimates, not medical assessments.
  • Analyze meals on request: when you explicitly choose AI analysis, send the selected photo or text and relevant context to the analysis provider, return an estimate, and let you review it before saving.
  • Manage subscriptions: determine whether your account can use paid features, restore purchases, and provide purchase support.
  • Keep services reliable: troubleshoot errors, limit abuse, maintain security and respond to requests.
  • Meet legal obligations: retain or disclose limited information where law requires it.

We do not sell meal photos, body measurements, or account data to advertisers. We do not use meal photos for advertising. If marketing or optional analytics are added, they require a separate product and policy review.

4. Providers and disclosures

FitJar uses service providers only for specific functions. Supabase supports account authentication, database storage, private meal-image storage and server functions. OpenAI receives a selected meal photo or text only when you request AI meal analysis, after the in-app disclosure. RevenueCat supports subscription entitlements and purchase status. Google Play processes Android purchases and may process Google sign-in; the applicable store controls payment and refund records. Vercel is the planned website host and may process web request logs. The site loads Google Fonts, so a visitor's browser requests font files from Google. Providers may process data in countries outside India.

We may disclose information where required by valid law, to protect users or our service, or during a transfer of the service subject to appropriate notice and safeguards. Provider processing terms and exact data locations must be confirmed before launch. Review provider policies directly for their own processing.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Review and correct profile information and logged entries in the app where those controls are available.
  • Choose whether to use optional AI meal analysis. You can log meals without submitting a photo for analysis.
  • Delete individual meals where available, or request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Ask for access, correction, export, withdrawal of consent, or information about processing at fitjar.support@gmail.com. We will verify that a request belongs to the account holder before disclosing or changing personal information.
  • Manage Google Play subscriptions separately in Google Play Subscriptions.

Some requests may be limited by applicable law or technical constraints; we will explain a decision where appropriate. Withdrawing consent for data needed to run the account may require account deletion.

6. Retention and deletion

Account records are generally kept while the account remains active. FitJar's in-app deletion flow asks the backend to remove private meal images and then delete the authenticated account; associated database records are intended to be removed through database relationships. You can also request deletion through the web request instructions. Deleting the app from your phone does not delete a cloud account. Deleting an account does not automatically cancel a Google Play subscription.

Important pre-launch gap: exact production periods for failed meal-photo cleanup, backups, diagnostic logs, AI-provider processing, and deletion request records are not finalized or verified. We will publish concrete periods and test deletion across those systems before launch. Some information may need to be retained for legal, security or financial reasons; any such exception will be limited and disclosed.

7. Children and younger users

FitJar's current product design allows users aged 16 and above and blocks AI meal analysis for users under 18. It is not intended for children under 16. Age checks in the app are not a substitute for a verified consent and safety process. Before allowing 16- and 17-year-olds to enroll in India, FitJar must complete legal review of parental consent requirements and clinical review of age-appropriate nutrition goals. If those safeguards are not ready, the launch age must be raised to 18 and enforced across account creation and backend access.

If you believe someone under the minimum permitted age has provided information, email us so we can investigate and remove it where appropriate.

8. Security, transfers and limitations

FitJar uses authenticated access, private storage for meal photos, access rules for account records, and secure transport to protect data. Local device storage and third-party services also carry risks; no system can promise absolute security. Report suspected account or data problems to fitjar.support@gmail.com.

Service providers may handle data outside India. International transfer rules and provider contracts must be reviewed before distribution expands beyond India.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when features, providers, laws or practices change. Material changes will be surfaced through the app or this website before they apply where required. The date at the top identifies the current draft. Contact: fitjar.support@gmail.com.
