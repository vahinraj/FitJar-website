Source model

The anatomy derivative is based on the muscle-only export from the Z-Anatomy project and the original BodyParts3D model by Kousaku Okubo and the Database Center for Life Science. The browser export and simplification source is hpfrei/body-anatomy-3d-viewer.

FitJar modifications

FitJar retained muscle geometry, removed bones, organs, nerves, vessels and descriptive metadata, grouped geometry into fitness regions, simplified and welded the geometry, normalized scale, and replaced the materials. The website payload is further simplified for an efficient public preview. It does not expose the app's muscle selection or zoom interaction.

License

The modified anatomy model remains available under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International. No endorsement by the original authors is implied. The full source attribution is also recorded in the FitJar app at assets/anatomy/ATTRIBUTION.md.

Male and female previews

The preview's Male and Female controls show the same licensed full-body anatomy with two presentation proportions so visitors can explore both FitJar profile directions. The preview includes the Blender face and soft-tissue overlays used by FitJar as visual-only layers. Their exact MPFB version and donor asset-pack provenance still needs to be confirmed before a public commercial launch.
