ACCOUNT CONTROLS

Delete your account.

You can request deletion of your FitJar account and associated data from inside the app or by contacting us from the email linked to your account.

For FitJar users in India · Last updated 27 September 2026
ON THIS PAGEDelete in the appRequest by emailWhat is removedSubscriptionsNeed help?

Before you delete: account deletion is intended to be permanent. Save anything you want to keep first. Google Play subscriptions must be canceled separately.

Delete inside FitJar

  1. Open FitJar and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Profile, then choose Delete Account.
  3. Read the confirmation and complete the deletion action. You may be asked to sign in again for security.

The current app sends an authenticated deletion request to FitJar's backend. The backend attempts to remove private meal images and delete the account. If the request fails, the app should show an error rather than claiming success. Contact support if that happens.

Request deletion without the app

Email fitjar.support@gmail.com with the subject FitJar account deletion request. Send the message from the email address linked to your FitJar account if possible. You do not need to send your password, payment details, meal photos, or identity documents in the first message.

We will reply with an account-verification step before deleting data. If you no longer have access to that email address, explain this in the message; a different verification method may be needed. We will confirm the outcome to you. The web page does not require you to reinstall the app.

What deletion covers

The intended deletion scope includes your FitJar sign-in account, profile, saved meals, workout and progress records, cloud backup records and private meal images associated with the account. Records on a device that remains offline may require app data removal from that device. Limited records may be retained where law requires them or for necessary security and dispute handling.

Pre-launch verification still required: the production deletion process, database cascades, backup expiration, provider retention and exact completion times need end-to-end testing and published periods. See the Privacy Policy retention section for current status.

Cancel a Google Play subscription separately

Account deletion and app uninstall do not cancel a Google Play subscription. If you purchased a plan through Google Play, open Google Play Subscriptions, select FitJar when listed, and choose Cancel subscription. Google Play controls the actual billing status and refund process.

Need help?

Email fitjar.support@gmail.com. We can help with a failed deletion, access issue, or a question about retained data. Do not include passwords or full payment card numbers in support email.
