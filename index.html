Want to know more? Talk to us ↗

Is FitJar a medical app?+

No. FitJar is for general fitness and wellness. Nutrition values, recovery scores, and Body Intelligence views are estimates, not diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice. Read the health disclaimer.

Does FitJar analyze every meal photo automatically?+

No. Analysis starts only when you choose to analyze a photo or text entry. You review the estimate before saving a meal.

Can I use FitJar without a subscription?+

FitJar is designed with a usable free tier. Paid plans are in preparation and will show current prices and terms in the app before any purchase.

How do I manage a subscription?+

When subscriptions launch, purchases made through Google Play will be managed in Google Play Subscriptions.

How do I delete my account?+

Use the in-app account deletion control or follow the steps on our account deletion page.
