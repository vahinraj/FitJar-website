THE DETAILS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Is FitJar a medical app?+

No. FitJar is for general fitness and wellness. Nutrition values, recovery scores and Body Intelligence views are estimates and are not diagnosis, treatment or medical advice. See the health disclaimer.

Does FitJar analyze every meal photo automatically?+

No. AI meal analysis starts only when you choose to analyze a photo or text entry. The app presents the processing disclosure before that action. You review the estimate before saving a meal.

Can I use FitJar without a subscription?+

FitJar is designed with a usable free tier. Paid plans are being prepared and will add higher limits and deeper views. The in-app offer will show current prices and terms before any purchase.

How do I manage or cancel a subscription?+

When subscriptions launch, purchases made through Google Play will be managed in Google Play Subscriptions. Deleting your FitJar account does not itself cancel a store subscription.

How do I delete my account?+

Use the in-app account deletion control, or follow the instructions on our account deletion page. We will verify requests made outside the app before acting.
