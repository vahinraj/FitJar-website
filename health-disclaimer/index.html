Health Disclaimer.

FitJar helps you reflect on activity and nutrition. Its insights have limits, and your health deserves qualified care when needed.

Last updated 27 September 2026 · Pre-launch draft
ON THIS PAGENot medical careEstimates and AIExercise safetyNutrition safetyEmergencies

1. General wellness only

FitJar is not a medical device or healthcare provider. It does not diagnose, prevent, monitor, treat or cure any condition. Content and calculations do not replace advice from a physician, registered dietitian or other qualified professional. Do not delay or ignore professional advice because of information shown in the app.

2. Estimates can be wrong

AI meal analysis may miss ingredients, allergens, preparation methods or portions. Calories, nutrients, activity expenditure, recovery and training-load views are estimates based on incomplete or self-reported information. A 3D body visualization shows training data patterns; it is not a scan of your body or a measure of injury, physiology or medical recovery. Check outputs before acting on them.

3. Exercise safely

Exercise carries risks, including injury. Choose activities appropriate for your ability and environment. Consult a qualified professional before beginning or changing a program if you have a condition, injury, are pregnant, or have other concerns. Stop and seek medical guidance if you experience pain, dizziness, chest discomfort or other concerning symptoms.

4. Nutrition and younger users

Do not use a FitJar estimate as the sole basis for restricting food, managing a medical condition or treating an eating disorder. The current adult calorie formula and weight-loss targets have not been clinically validated for people under 18. AI meal analysis is blocked for users under 18 while additional safeguards are reviewed. Anyone with specialized dietary needs should seek professional guidance.

5. Emergencies and questions

FitJar does not monitor emergencies. If you think you have a medical emergency, contact local emergency services immediately. For product questions, email fitjar.support@gmail.com. See the Terms and Privacy Policy.
